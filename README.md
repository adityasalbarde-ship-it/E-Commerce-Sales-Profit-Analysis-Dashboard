# E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, payment methods, and monthly trends. An interactive Excel dashboard was created to present key performance indicators, trends, and business insights in an easy-to-understand format.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and identify patterns in sales, profit, products, categories, cities, states, customers, payment methods, and monthly performance. The analysis aims to support data-driven business decisions by highlighting strong-performing areas and opportunities for improvement.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 1,000 e-commerce orders with information about order dates, customers, cities, states, product categories, products, quantities, unit prices, discounts, sales, profit, and payment methods. The data was cleaned and validated in Excel before performing the analysis.

## Data Cleaning & Validation

The dataset was reviewed and cleaned in Excel before analysis. The cleaning process included checking for missing values, correcting spelling inconsistencies in city and state names, identifying duplicate Order IDs, standardizing date formats, and validating sales calculations. These steps helped improve data consistency and reliability before creating PivotTables, charts, and the dashboard.

## Analysis Performed

The project uses Excel PivotTables and PivotCharts to analyze:

- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Sales and profit by category
- Sales and profit by state
- Sales and profit by city
- Product-level sales, profit, and quantity
- Customer-level sales and profit
- Sales by payment method
- Orders by city
- Average Order Value by city
- Profit margin trends

An interactive dashboard was also developed using KPI cards and slicers for Category and State to make the analysis easier to explore.

## Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to provide a clear overview of e-commerce performance. The dashboard includes KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Quantity, and Average Order Value, along with charts showing monthly performance, category and state sales, product and customer performance, payment methods, and profit margin trends.

Category and State slicers allow users to interactively filter the dashboard and explore the data from different perspectives.

## Key Insights

- Home & Kitchen was the highest-selling category, generating approximately ₹12.56 lakh in sales.
- Electronics was the second-highest category, with approximately ₹11.85 lakh in sales.
- Clothing generated approximately ₹9.30 lakh, showing solid category performance.
- Books had the lowest category sales at approximately ₹4.88 lakh, indicating an opportunity for further investigation.
- July recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately ₹3.84 lakh.
- April recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately ₹2.59 lakh.
- Debit Card was the highest-selling payment method, with approximately ₹8.48 lakh in sales.
- Overall, the dataset contained 1,000 orders, 2,998 units sold, and approximately ₹38.60 lakh in total sales.

## Recommendations

- Maintain strong inventory availability for Home & Kitchen and Electronics, as these categories generate the highest sales.
- Investigate the lower sales performance of Books through product selection, pricing, and promotional strategies.
- Analyze the factors behind July's strong sales and consider applying successful strategies during weaker months.
- Use targeted promotions during low-performing periods such as April to improve sales.
- Continue supporting multiple payment methods to provide customers with convenient checkout options.
- Increase Average Order Value through product bundles, cross-selling, and relevant add-on recommendations.

## Project Outcome

This project strengthened practical skills in Excel data cleaning, data analysis, PivotTables, PivotCharts, dashboard development, data visualization, and business insight generation. The analysis transforms raw e-commerce data into an interactive dashboard and actionable recommendations that can support business decision-making.
